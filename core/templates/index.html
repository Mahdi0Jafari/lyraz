About Lyraz Live Hub

Lyraz (https://lyraz.ir) is a decentralized web audio player and live collaborative music platform. It enables zero-latency synchronized audio streaming across multiple screens, including mobile devices, desktops, and Smart TVs.

Key Features of Lyraz

  • Zero-Latency Synchronization: Stream music synchronously across connected screens using Server-Sent Events (SSE).
  • High-Resolution Audio: Stream lossless audio at 320kbps and 192kbps bitrates.
  • Synchronized Real-Time Lyrics: Dynamic, millisecond-accurate lyrics rendering with hardware-accelerated ambient glow.
  • Telegram Bot Vault (@LyrazBot): Instantly queue and download tracks and Spotify playlists via Telegram.
  • Curated Music Magazine (@LyrazMusic): Discover hand-picked tracks, timeless sounds, and audio stories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lyraz?

Lyraz is a high-resolution synchronized music streaming platform and web audio hub for personal and party listening.

How do I connect a device?

Scan the QR code displayed on the screen using your mobile phone or Telegram camera to connect and control playback.

How does the Telegram bot work?

Send any Spotify or YouTube music link to @LyrazBot on Telegram. The audio is instantly processed and streamed live on your connected Lyraz Web Hub screens.
