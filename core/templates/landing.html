Under the Hood

Built on high-performance distributed streaming standards used by modern audio titans.

timer

Atomic Clock Synchronization (PTP)

Our Network Time Protocol calibration ensures multi-room and multi-device audio play within milliseconds of each other. No echo, no drift.

smartphone

Host Mini-App Remote Control

The session owner receives a dedicated Telegram Mini-App remote with instant zero-latency optimistic track skipping, volume control, and seek scrubbing.

Hardware-Accelerated Live Lyrics

Dynamic time-coded synced lyrics render on the big screen with ambient neon glows, matching the vocalist note-for-note.

Lossless Audio Pipeline

Direct streaming pipeline supporting lossless 320kbps and 192kbps audio with automated background normalization and zero transcoding degradation.
