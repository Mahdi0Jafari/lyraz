Frictionless Experience

How Lyraz Works

Three effortless steps to transform any screen into a collaborative soundstage.

01

Open the Screen

Open lyraz.ir on your Smart TV, laptop, or projector. A permanent room hub with an instant QR code appears.

Works on any modern browser
02

Scan & Connect

Guests scan the QR code with their phone camera. Telegram opens immediately with @LyrazBot linked to the session.

Powered by @LyrazBot
03

Queue & Enjoy

Drop any audio file, voice note, or Spotify track link in chat. The system streams it in lossless 320kbps with real-time lyrics on the main screen.
